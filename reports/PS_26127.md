# SIH 2026 Problem Statement Report

## 1. Problem Statement Overview

- **Problem ID:** 26127
- **Problem Title:** City-wide ANPR and Trajectory Tracking Platform
- **Organization:** Ministry of Home Affairs / Police (Implied)
- **Category:** Software
- **Theme:** Smart Cities / Security
- **Difficulty Level:** Medium / Hard

### Problem Statement

> Modern urban centers deploy vast networks of CCTV and ANPR cameras. However, most systems process feeds in isolated silos. This lack of integration prevents city authorities from automatically tracking high-interest vehicles across different sectors and limits macro-level traffic analysis. The objective is to develop a robust, centralized AI software platform that processes multi-camera feeds to achieve >90% OCR accuracy, reconstructs complete vehicle travel trajectories on a GIS map, and performs macro traffic flow analytics (heatmaps, bottlenecks).

### One-Line Summary

A centralized AI software that connects all city traffic cameras to track a specific car's entire journey on a map and analyze overall city traffic jams.

---

## 2. Problem Understanding

### What is the actual problem?

Police have thousands of cameras, but they don't talk to each other. If a criminal's car passes Camera A, then Camera B, then Camera C, the police currently have to manually check each camera's logs to guess where the car went.

### Who faces this problem?

Traffic Police, City Municipal Corporations, Law Enforcement.

### Why is this problem important?

Crucial for tracking stolen vehicles, fleeing suspects, and understanding city-wide traffic congestion to improve urban planning.

### Existing Problems / Limitations

Current ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition) just reads the plate and saves it in a local database. There is no spatial-temporal mapping (linking time and physical location across the city).

---

## 3. Expected Solution

### What is SIH asking us to build?

An enterprise-grade software platform with three parts:
1. High-accuracy ANPR/OCR engine that works in bad weather and angles.
2. A trajectory engine where police can type a license plate, and it draws the car's path on a Google Map.
3. A traffic analytics dashboard showing city-wide heatmaps of congestion.

### Proposed Solution Concept

A central cloud backend. We simulate 4-5 "camera nodes" sending video streams. The backend runs a YOLO+LPRNet model to detect plates. The detected plates, along with the camera's GPS coordinates and timestamp, are saved to a graph database (like Neo4j) or spatial database (PostGIS). A React frontend with Mapbox/Leaflet displays the map and draws lines between camera points for a searched vehicle.

### Key Features

- Deep learning based OCR (>90% accuracy).
- GIS map integration showing chronological paths.
- Heatmaps of vehicle density.
- Real-time alerts for blacklisted vehicles.

### AI / ML Requirements

- Is AI/ML required? Yes, heavy computer vision.
- What models could be used? YOLOv8 for vehicle/plate detection; LPRNet for reading Indian license plates.
- Is training required? Yes, fine-tuning on Indian license plates in bad lighting.
- Are pretrained models available? Yes, many ANPR models exist but need tuning.

## 8. MVP Feasibility for SIH

### Can we build an MVP during the hackathon?

**Verdict:** Yes

### MVP Scope

A web application. We upload 3 different pre-recorded traffic videos, assigning each a fake GPS location on a map. The backend processes them simultaneously. The user types a license plate number (which we know is in the videos), and the app draws a line on the map connecting the 3 locations in chronological order.

## 12. Risks and Challenges

| Risk / Challenge | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| OCR Accuracy is too low | High | Use Image Enhancement (Super Resolution, Contrast adjustment) before passing to OCR. |
| Video processing lag | Medium | Skip frames (process 5 frames per second instead of 30). |

### Biggest Technical Risk

The ML model being too slow to process multiple video streams simultaneously in real-time.

### Biggest Project Risk

Failing to integrate the ML output with the spatial mapping frontend.
