# SIH 2026 Problem Statement Report

## 1. Problem Statement Overview

- **Problem ID:** 26008
- **Problem Title:** Intelligent Conveyor Belt Health Monitoring and Predictive Maintenance System
- **Organization:** NMDC Limited
- **Category:** Hardware / Software
- **Theme:** Smart Automation / Industry 4.0
- **Difficulty Level:** Medium / Hard

### Problem Statement

> In the iron ore mining industry, conveyor belt systems are the backbone of material transportation. Belt joints are highly vulnerable to failure due to excessive tension, misalignment, wear, overloading, and maintenance deficiencies. Unexpected belt failures cause downtime, safety risks, and high costs. Traditional maintenance is reactive. There is a need for an intelligent, real-time, predictive system using IoT, AI, and ML to detect early signs of belt joint deterioration and prevent catastrophic failures.

### One-Line Summary

An IoT and AI-based system to monitor the health of mining conveyor belts in real-time and predict failures before they happen.

---

## 2. Problem Understanding

### What is the actual problem?

Conveyor belts in mines move thousands of tons of heavy rocks. Over time, the joints connecting the belt segments tear, wear out, or misalign. Currently, maintenance teams wait until a belt breaks to fix it, which stops the entire mine's production and costs a lot of money.

### Who faces this problem?

Mining companies (NMDC), plant maintenance engineers, and operational managers.

### Why is this problem important?

A broken conveyor belt halts ore transportation entirely. Predictive maintenance saves millions of rupees in downtime and prevents dangerous accidents caused by snapping belts under high tension.

### Existing Problems / Limitations

Manual inspections are done periodically, missing sudden deteriorations. Reactive maintenance leads to unexpected shutdowns.

---

## 3. Expected Solution

### What is SIH asking us to build?

A digitalized system that integrates IoT sensors and AI cameras to continuously monitor the conveyor belt, identify cracks/wear, and predict when it will fail so maintenance can be scheduled proactively.

### Proposed Solution Concept

A sensor station mounted over a specific section of the conveyor belt. It includes a camera for visual crack detection (AI/ML), thermal sensors for overheating, and acoustic/vibration sensors for roller health. All data is sent to a cloud dashboard that alerts engineers.

### Key Features

- Real-time IoT sensor monitoring (vibration, temperature).
- Computer Vision to detect visible cracks or edge damage on the belt.
- Predictive ML models to estimate remaining useful life.
- Alerting system for abnormal conditions (e.g., misalignment).

## 5. Software Requirements

| Component | Technology / Framework | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Edge AI Script | Python (OpenCV/TensorFlow) | Image processing for cracks |
| IoT Gateway | Python / MQTT | Send sensor data to cloud |
| Web Dashboard | React / Node.js / Grafana | Display health & alerts |

### AI / ML Requirements

- Is AI/ML required? Yes, highly expected for computer vision.
- What models could be used? YOLO for crack/defect detection, or standard CNNs.
- Is training required? Yes, on images of damaged vs healthy belts.
- Are pretrained models available? General defect detection models exist, but fine-tuning on belt textures is needed.

### Software Complexity

**Level:** Medium / High

## 8. MVP Feasibility for SIH

### Can we build an MVP during the hackathon?

**Verdict:** Yes

### MVP Scope

A 1-2 foot long motorized toy/custom conveyor belt. Mount a camera over it and attach vibration sensors to the motor. As the belt runs, the dashboard shows normal. When a piece of the belt with a drawn/cut "crack" passes by, the AI detects it, stops the belt, and sends an alert.

### What should NOT be attempted during the hackathon?

- Drones for inspection (too complex and dangerous indoors).
- Full "Digital Twin" 3D physics simulation (a simple 2D dashboard is enough).

### Estimated Development Difficulty

**Level:** Medium

## 12. Risks and Challenges

| Risk / Challenge | Severity | Mitigation |

| AI model doesn't work on the physical demo belt | High | Make the "crack" on the demo belt very obvious (e.g., white paint on black belt) to ensure detection works perfectly. |
