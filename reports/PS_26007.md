# SIH 2026 Problem Statement Report

## 1. Problem Statement Overview

- **Problem ID:** 26007
- **Problem Title:** Intelligent Solution for Safe Movement of Mine Vehicles under Low-Visibility Conditions
- **Organization:** NMDC Limited
- **Category:** Hardware / Software
- **Theme:** Smart Automation / Mining
- **Difficulty Level:** Medium / Hard

### Problem Statement

> Dense fog and extremely low visibility during the monsoon season create major operational and safety challenges in the Bailadila iron ore mines. Poor visibility restricts dumper movement, forcing operators to reduce speed or temporarily halt operations to avoid accidents and unsafe conditions.This results in increased haul cycle times, reduced fleet productivity, lower ore evacuation, and production losses. The risk of vehicle collision, road accidents, and operational disruptions also increases substantially during such conditions. Existing visibility aids and operational controls have limited effectiveness in dense fog environments.There is a need for an intelligent, reliable, and technology-driven solution that can enable safe and efficient movement of mine vehicles under low-visibility conditions while ensuring continuity of operations and maintaining production levels.

### One-Line Summary

An intelligent sensor and communication system to help heavy mining vehicles navigate and avoid collisions safely during dense fog.

---

## 2. Problem Understanding

### What is the actual problem?

In large open-cast iron ore mines, heavy dumpers transport ore. During the monsoon, dense fog reduces visibility to 3-5 meters. Drivers cannot see the road or other vehicles, leading to accidents, forced halts, and massive production losses.

### Who faces this problem?

NMDC Limited, mining operators, dumper drivers, and mine safety managers.

### Why is this problem important?

Halting operations leads to huge financial losses due to reduced ore evacuation. Driving in fog without assistance risks fatal accidents and equipment damage.

### Existing Problems / Limitations

Current visibility aids (like standard fog lights or reflectors) are insufficient in dense, 3-meter visibility fog. Manual spotting is dangerous and inefficient.

---

## 3. Expected Solution

### What is SIH asking us to build?

A technology-driven system to improve driver situational awareness, provide vehicle guidance, avoid collisions, and allow a central control room to monitor fleet movement in real-time.

### Proposed Solution Concept

A multi-sensor hardware node mounted on each vehicle containing GPS, LiDAR/Radar, and V2V communication modules. A dashboard inside the cabin gives the driver a "virtual view" of the road and nearby vehicles. A central server tracks all vehicles via IoT.

### Key Features

- Real-time vehicle tracking using high-precision GPS.
- Proximity alerts and collision avoidance using Radar/LiDAR.
- Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communication to broadcast positions.
- In-cabin driver assistance dashboard (visual and audio alerts).
- Centralized fleet monitoring dashboard.

## 12. Risks and Challenges

| Risk / Challenge | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Inaccurate GPS indoors | High | Use manual joystick control or predefined paths for the demo, or test outside. |
| V2V Communication delay | Medium | Use fast protocols like ESP-NOW or NRF24 instead of Wi-Fi. |
| Demo failure due to loose wires | Medium | Solder components to a perfboard instead of using breadboards. |

### Biggest Technical Risk

Standard GPS modules won't work well inside a hackathon hall for the demo.

### Biggest Project Risk

Spending too much time on advanced AI vision and failing to get the basic hardware communication working.
