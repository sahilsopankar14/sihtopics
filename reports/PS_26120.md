# SIH 2026 Problem Statement Report

## 1. Problem Statement Overview

- **Problem ID:** 26120
- **Problem Title:** AI-enabled Well-to-Surface Digital Twin for Heavy Oil Recovery
- **Organization:** Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (Implied)
- **Category:** Software
- **Theme:** Energy / Petroleum
- **Difficulty Level:** Very Hard

### Problem Statement

> Baghewala Field produces heavy crude oil. The reservoir is characterized by high crude viscosity, low pressure, and low temperature. Artificial lift and thermal enhanced oil recovery (steam injection) are critical. Currently, Steam injection (CSS) and Sucker Rod Pump (SRP) operations are optimized separately using historical experience. There is a need for an integrated, data-driven AI Digital Twin that continuously optimizes both CSS and artificial lift operations to prevent rod floating, rod failures, and reduce energy consumption.

### One-Line Summary

An AI software system that simulates and optimizes steam injection and pumping mechanics for heavy crude oil wells.

---

## 2. Problem Understanding

### What is the actual problem?

Extracting heavy, thick oil is hard. They inject hot steam to melt it, then pump it out. Currently, engineers manually guess how much steam to inject and how fast to pump. If they pump too fast as the oil cools and thickens, the metal pump rod breaks (rod failure). If they inject too much steam, it wastes energy. They want AI to optimize both simultaneously.

### Who faces this problem?

Oil and gas extraction companies, petroleum engineers.

### Why is this problem important?

Broken pumps halt oil production and require extremely expensive repairs. Optimizing steam saves massive amounts of energy.

### Existing Problems / Limitations

Operations are manual, reactive, and siloed (the steam team doesn't optimize perfectly with the pumping team).

---

## 3. Expected Solution

### What is SIH asking us to build?

An AI-enabled Digital Twin of the oil well. It takes in production history, well temperature, and pump speed. It uses this to predict how the oil is heating/cooling and automatically recommends the perfect steam volume and pump speed (SPM) to avoid mechanical failure.

### Proposed Solution Concept

A web-based dashboard for petroleum engineers. It ingests CSV data of oil well parameters. An ML model (trained on historical well data) predicts the viscosity of the oil at current temperatures and suggests safe VFD (Variable Frequency Drive) settings for the pump.

### Key Features

- Optimize CSS cycle parameters (steam volume, soak time).
- Predict pump efficiency and rod floating based on temperature.
- Real-time dashboard with alerts for predicted equipment failure.
